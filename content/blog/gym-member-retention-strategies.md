Most gym owners think churn is a pricing problem. It isn't. Members who feel like a number in your spreadsheet leave. Members who feel like the gym actually knows them stay — even when there's a cheaper one down the street. This pillar piece is about the seven specific things you can do this month to drop your churn rate by 10-20%.

## Tactic 2: First-week onboarding ritual

TODO: 200 words. Most members lapse in their first 30 days. Tactical fixes:
- Day 1 welcome WhatsApp from the trainer
- Day 7 check-in: "How was your first week? Any questions?"
- Day 14 progress photo or measurement
- Day 30 plan review with a trainer

This costs ~5 minutes/week of staff time per new member. Big payoff.

## Tactic 4: Win-back sequence for lapsed members

TODO: 200 words. Members who lapsed 30-60 days ago are still gettable. Sequence:
- Day 30 post-lapse: friendly "we miss you" message
- Day 45: re-join offer (1 month free or no admission fee)
- Day 60: feedback request — "what could we have done better?"

The feedback ask is critical. Even from non-returning members, the data is gold.
